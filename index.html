Google Sheets Live Bi-Directional Sync

Connect your Google Sheet so all tenants, rent, electricity, and water meter updates sync live to your Google Sheet automatically!

Open this link on your mobile phone once to automatically connect Google Sheets without entering the URL again!

How to get your Web App URL (Takes 1 minute):
  1. Open your Google Sheet -> Click Extensions -> Apps Script.
  2. Paste the code from googleAppsScript.gs in this repository.
  3. Click Deploy -> New deployment (Select Web app, Access: Anyone).
  4. Copy the Web App URL and paste it above!
